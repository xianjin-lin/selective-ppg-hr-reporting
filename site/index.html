How it works
  • Within AF · matched count: each score reports the same number of AF windows (its top-k among AF only). This isolates accuracy.
  • Shared budget: all 240 windows are ranked together and the top-k are reported. This measures allocation across rhythms.
  • A failure is an estimate more than 5 bpm from the reference, or a missing one. Ties are broken by window order, as in the released analysis_core.retained.
  • Synthetic data from examples/run_demo.py (demo-A, seed 0). The continuous budget sweep is a software illustration; the manuscript evaluates two pre-specified operating points on patient data.
