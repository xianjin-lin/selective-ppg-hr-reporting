# Aggregate result snapshots

These CSVs are fixed aggregate outputs from the manuscript analysis, not
outputs generated by the synthetic demo. No window-level prediction, patient
identifier, waveform, clinical table or access credential is present.

## vitaldb_primary_contrasts.csv

Forty original additional-cohort confidence-minus-quality contrasts, across
five configurations and two global operating points, plus within-rhythm
matched-count comparisons. Columns include endpoint, configuration, label,
three seed-specific estimates, seed-averaged estimate, family membership,
exclusion status, simultaneous intervals and marginal intervals.

- E1: retained failure-rate difference in percentage points;
- E2a: AF/AFL coverage difference in percentage points;
- E2b: reliable AF/AFL output-count difference;
- E3: overall reliable-output-count difference.

F1 jointly covers E1/E2a/E2b; F2 covers E3. G-T uses the MIMIC TEST-derived
retention fraction and G-D the development-derived fraction, applied to the
additional VitalDB cohort. Labels are analysis strata, not inferred diagnoses.

## mimic_global_intervals.csv

Twenty global MIMIC confidence-minus-quality coverage/reliable-output
contrasts, with nominal and simultaneous intervals. Endpoint units are stated
in the `outcome` field. MIMIC TEST was previously examined and is developmental
evidence, not an independent holdout confirmation.

## Reading these files

An interval spanning zero is imprecise evidence, not equivalence. Sign alone
is not an overall quality judgment: a policy can improve retained failure or
overall reliable yield while reducing rhythm-specific coverage and yield.
`category` labels in the historical VitalDB output describe the frozen
directional comparison, not new hypothesis tests by this repository.

These files allow inspection of reported aggregate results. They do not contain
the subject-level bootstrap vectors or individual predictions required to
recompute all intervals; those inputs are not redistributed in this partial
release. See the manuscript and supplement for the complete study specification.
